# Workshop Equipment Loans — review notes

## Confirmed scope and data handling

- Prepare a local Foundation Plan and sample proposal for review; do not submit or Compile.
- Public local workshop demo with no Accounts. Borrowers are ordinary domain records, not login identities.
- The preview may use fully synthetic data while retaining repeated borrowers, repeated equipment, and returned and outstanding Loans.
- Keep supplied originals and any re-identification mapping out of this planning root, later design archives, seeds, and repositories. The supplied artifact remains outside this root; it was not copied or altered. No mapping was created.

## Proposed choices awaiting model review

- One Equipment record represents one tagged physical item; one Borrower record represents one person; one Loan represents one borrowing episode. This candidate has no quantity inventory.
- Require Equipment tag/name, Borrower name, Loan equipment/borrower, and checkout date. Email, due date, return date, and workshop note are optional. These are proposed rules, not deductions from populated or blank cells.
- Restrict deleting an Equipment or Borrower referenced by a Loan, to preserve its history. No uniqueness, identity matching, or immutability rule is asserted from this small artifact.
- Start the demo on Loans, with public list/detail pages for all three Entities and related Loans on Equipment/Borrower detail. Editing, checkout, and return actions are deferred pending workflow choices.
- The existing staff-note concept is presented as a Workshop note containing only invented, harmless demo text. It has no private staff-only access in this public demo.
- All development records are disposable examples, not all-environment reference data. The dates are fixed historical examples. A missing return date represents an outstanding sample Loan; it is not a dynamic claim about today.

## Open product questions

- Must Equipment tags be unique? May Borrowers share a name or email, and what identifies a person when creating a later Loan?
- Is a due date always required? What date-ordering rules should be enforced?
- May an item have overlapping outstanding Loans, and are editing, returning, and deleting Loans required for the workshop?

## Requirements outside the Plan vocabulary

No custom import workflow, automated reminder, or other application behavior outside the Plan vocabulary has been agreed. The CSV is a modeling example, not a request for an importer. Data-handling constraints above must be carried into any later implementation handoff.
