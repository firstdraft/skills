I propose three Entities, with **one Loan per item per borrowing episode**:

| Entity | Proposed stored facts |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment | Equipment tag and name |
| Member | Name and contact email |
| Loan | Equipment, Member, checkout date, due date, optional return date, optional staff note |

A Member can have many Loans, and Equipment can have many Loans over time. Returning equipment closes that Loan; borrowing it again creates another. A blank return date means **outstanding**, derived directly from the date rather than maintained as a separate status. Member names and email addresses are not assumed unique.

The main clarifications are:

1. **Inventory and identity:** Does a tag identify one physical item, do you also lend interchangeable stock by quantity, or both? How should staff distinguish members with the same name or changed email?
2. **Loan rules:** Is only one outstanding Loan allowed per item, and must every Loan have a due date? Calendar dates, renewals, and correction of history also need agreement.
3. **Access:** Is this staff-only, or can members manage their own loans? Who may see contact details and staff notes, and which clients do you want?
