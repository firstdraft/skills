The structured model is:

| Record | What it represents |
| --- | --- |
| Borrower | A person and optional contact email; reusing this record across loans is a proposal. |
| Equipment | One individual physical item, with a name and optional asset tag. |
| Loan | One borrowing transaction, linked to its borrower, with a reference, checkout date, and optional staff note. |
| Loan item | One particular item on one loan, with its own optional return date and condition. Other items can remain outstanding. |
| Damage photo | One image and its caption, linked to a loan item. A collection allows any number of photos, including more than the paper form's four boxes. |
