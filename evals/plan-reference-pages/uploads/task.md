4. Visitors love extra pictures: staff should be able to add any number of gallery photos to a pet, each with a short caption, from the pet's page.
5. Photos must be JPEG or PNG and no bigger than 5 MB.
6. Add two example pets, Biscuit the dog and Miso the cat, with their real photos from our shelter folder.
7. We'll try it on our laptops first and then deploy. What do we need to set up for the uploads to work?"
