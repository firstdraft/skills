"Our project tracker is wide open: every page is public. Please lock it down.
1. Nobody sees anything without signing in.
2. A project is visible to its owner and to its members (people with a membership in it). Only the owner can rename it.
3. Tasks are visible to everyone who can see their project. Any of those people can add a task, and the task records
   who added it.
4. A task can be changed by the person who added it or by the project's owner.
5. Only admins can delete a project.
6. Admins should also be able to rename any project.
7. Whoever creates a project becomes its owner."
