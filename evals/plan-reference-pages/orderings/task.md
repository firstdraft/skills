"Our help desk app needs its tickets in a sensible order.
1. The tickets list should show the most severe tickets first (Critical, then High, Normal, Low). Among tickets of the same severity, the one entered first comes first.
2. On a board's page, its tickets should be listed alphabetically by subject.
3. Our weekly report code needs the tickets that were closed most recently first, with tickets that are still open at the end. No page needs this yet.
4. When our code asks for a board's first or last ticket, it should go by the date the ticket was reported.
5. We'd also like a list of tickets by due date, soonest first, with tickets that have no due date at the end.
6. Let people click a column heading on the tickets list to sort by that column."
