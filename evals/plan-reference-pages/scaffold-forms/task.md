"Our recipe box app needs its forms.
1. A signed-in person can create a cookbook by giving it a name. The cookbook belongs to whoever created it, and only that person can rename or delete it.
2. From a cookbook's page, its owner can add a recipe with a title and instructions. After saving, take them back to the cookbook's page.
3. Record the date each recipe was added. Nobody types it; the app fills it in.
4. A recipe is either Original or Adapted. When it is Adapted, the person must say where it was adapted from.
5. Every new recipe starts as Untested. When editing a recipe, its owner can mark it Tested, and then they must give the date they tested it.
6. On a recipe's page, the cookbook's owner can edit the recipe and delete it. After deleting, go back to the cookbook's page.
7. When a recipe is added, email the cookbook's owner."
