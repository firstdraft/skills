"Our handbook app keeps articles in spaces. We want to organize each space's articles into an outline.
1. An article can sit under another article in the same space, as deep as people like. Articles with nothing above them are the top level of the space.
2. An article's page should show the article it sits under, list the articles directly under it, and have a way to add a new article right under it.
3. People can move an article under a different article, or back to the top level, by editing it.
4. When an article is deleted, the articles directly under it move up to the top level of the space.
5. An article must never end up under itself, directly or through the articles below it, or under an article from another space.
6. A space's page should list only its top-level articles.
7. Add example data: a space called Handbook with a top-level article "Onboarding" and an article "First week" under it."
