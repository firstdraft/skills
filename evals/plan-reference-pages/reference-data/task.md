"Our help desk needs ticket categories.
1. Every ticket is filed under a category: General, Billing, or Technical. These three must exist in production from the first day, with nobody having to type them in.
2. Each category has a short code that our reports use (general, billing, technical) and that never repeats, plus a one-line description.
3. When someone opens or edits a ticket they pick its category, and the ticket's page and the tickets list show it.
4. Add an example ticket for development: a customer who was charged twice, filed under Billing.
5. New tickets should start in General when nobody picks a category.
6. The tickets list should show only Billing tickets, since the billing team uses it most.
7. We may rename Billing to "Payments" next year. Tell us what that will take."
