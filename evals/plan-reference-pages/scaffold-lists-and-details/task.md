"Our community garden app lets members add and edit their plots. Now people need to browse them.
1. The plot list is the first page of the app. Anyone, even a visitor who isn't signed in, can browse it. It shows
   only active plots, in alphabetical order by name.
2. Each row of the list shows the plot's name, its size, and the gardener's name.
3. Opening a plot shows its name, size, notes, and gardener, and the plantings still growing in it, newest planting
   first. Each planting shows its crop and the date it was planted.
4. Only signed-in members can see a plot's plantings. Visitors still see the plot itself.
5. A member can open a planting on its own page, showing its crop, planted date, whether it's harvested, and its
   plot.
6. Put a search box above the plot list so people can find a plot by name.
7. On the plot list, also show each plot's three latest plantings under its row."
